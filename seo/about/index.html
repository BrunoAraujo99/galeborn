Galeborn, a free anime elemental duel game

Galeborn is a free 3D anime fighting game that runs straight in your browser, with no download or install. Pick a fighter who bends wind, fire, earth or water and settle it in fast, cinematic one-on-one duels.

Play free now
Runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari

Game modes

  • Online ranked
    Matchmaking against players around the world, with ranked tiers, seasons and a leaderboard.
  • Vs a friend
    Share a room code or invite link and duel a friend online.
  • Vs a bot
    Practise against AI opponents at three difficulty levels.
  • Training
    Six lessons that teach movement, combos, blocking, parrying and bursts.
  • Solo boss
    Face Varukh, the Cinder Warden, a two-phase fire boss.

Anime combat

Launchers, air combos, parries, guard breaks, beam struggles, ring-outs and slow-motion finishers, with anime impact frames and a cinematic camera. Each element has its own moveset and matchups.

Arenas

Lantern Strait at sunset, Thunderhead Spire with lightning strikes, and Frostbell Pass with blizzard gusts, each with breakable pillars.

How to play

Play with keyboard and mouse or a gamepad on any computer with a modern browser and WebGL. Galeborn is free to play: open the game and press Online, Vs a friend, Vs a bot, Training or Solo boss.

Questions

Is Galeborn free?
Yes. Galeborn is completely free to play in your browser.
Do I need to download anything?
No. The game runs in your browser. An optional offline file is also available from the title screen.
Can I play with friends?
Yes. Choose Vs a friend and share the room code or invite link.
